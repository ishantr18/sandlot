# Demo Video Script: Diamond Drive

*Draft, Sep 27, 2026. Target 2:45 (limit 1–3 min). Public on YouTube, unlisted is not enough, check the portal.
Narration is a starting point. Say it in your own words; judges can tell when it's read.*

**Setup before filming**
- Use the pristine **demo account** (clean history, 4+ sessions so the progress chart has a line).
- Phone screen recording on (iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings).
- Film one fresh side-view swing for the live demo. Good light, full body in frame.
- Quiet room for voiceover. Record narration separately if easier and lay it over the footage.

## Rules to keep it honest
- Only show pilot results if a pilot actually happened. Otherwise the progress chart is your own.
- No stock or YouTube swing footage in the submission video. Only your own or consenting players' clips.
- Show the app working live. Don't use mockups.
- Keep jargon out of narration ("computer vision" is fine; "WebAssembly" and "CNN" are for the interview).
