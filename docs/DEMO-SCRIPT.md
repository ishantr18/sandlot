# Demo Video Script: Diamond Drive

*Updated Oct 11, 2026. Target 2:50 (limit 1–3 min). Upload as **Public** on YouTube (check the portal —
unlisted may not count). Narration is a starting point: say it in your own words; judges can tell when
it's read.*

## Setup before filming
- **Demo account with 6+ analyzed swings** (I'll set this up): enough for every Growth bar to show a
  "since the start" change. Film them at **60 fps** so the hips-before-shoulders tile has a number.
- Before the coach shot, **confirm one skill as the coach** so the parent view shows "✓ Coach-confirmed".
- Three logins ready (passwords in `TEST-ACCOUNTS.local.md`): **player** (demo account), **coach**
  `ishant.ram.ai@gmail.com`, **parent** linked to the demo player.
- Phone screen recording on (iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings).
- One fresh **side-view** swing for the live shot: good light, whole body in frame, **only you in the
  frame** (a second person close to the camera can make the privacy check block the upload — that's
  the app doing its job, but not what you want mid-take).
- Quiet room for the voiceover. Record narration separately if easier and lay it over the footage.

## Rules to keep it honest
- **No pilot claims** — there wasn't one. The Growth bars are your own demo account's swings.
- No stock or YouTube swing footage in the video — only your own or consenting players' clips.
- Show the app working live — no mockups.
- Keep jargon out of the narration ("AI" and "3D" are fine; "WebAssembly", "CNN", "YuNet" are for the
  interview).
- Don't stage a "wrong camera angle gets rejected" moment unless you've tested that exact clip first —
  that check isn't reliable enough to count on.
