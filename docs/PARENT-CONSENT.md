# Diamond Drive: Information & Consent for Parents

*One-page handout for families trying Diamond Drive during testing (fall 2026).
Plain-language summary written by the student developer. This is not a legal document.*

---

**What it is.** Diamond Drive is a free app built by [STUDENT NAME], a student in
[SCHOOL], for the Congressional App Challenge. Your child films a baseball swing on a phone.
The app measures their mechanics and suggests drills. Volunteer coaches may add comments.

**What happens to the video**
- **Faces are pixelated on the phone before anything uploads.** The app then re-checks its
  own work. If it can't confirm the face is hidden, it won't upload.
- **Sound is removed.**
- **The original video never leaves the device.** Only the face-hidden copy and the
  swing measurements are stored.
- Stored videos are **private**. There's no public link; viewing needs a login and a link
  that expires after 15 minutes.
- No ads, no tracking tools, no data sold or shared. The AI runs on the phone, not on a
  company's server.

**Who can see what**

| Person | Sees |
|---|---|
| Your child | Their own swings, measurements, comments, drills |
| **You (parent account)** | **Everything your child sees, including every coach comment** |
| Volunteer coaches | The face-hidden swing and measurements. **Not your child's name**, only a code like "Player #7F3K" |
| Anyone else | Nothing |

**Safety rules built into the app**
- There are **no private messages**. Coaches can only leave comments on a swing and
  assign drills, and parents see all of it.
- Coaches can't contact your child through the app or see who they are.
- Any comment can be reported, and any coach can be blocked.

**Your choices**
- Participation is voluntary, and you can stop anytime.
- Ask us to delete your child's account and all their videos, and we will within 7 days.
- With your separate OK below, we may use **face-hidden** screenshots or progress charts in
  the competition video. We will never use your child's name.
