# Idea — Concept & Feature Set

## Core loop (works with ZERO coaches — solves cold-start)
1. Player films/uploads a swing clip (guided filming setup: side view, full body, phone propped)
2. Faces detected and blurred **on-device, before anything uploads**
3. Pose estimation overlays a skeleton; app computes 3–4 mechanics metrics
4. Plain-English findings ("your hips fire late") → matching drills auto-assigned
5. Metrics saved per session → progress charts over time

## Layer 2: Supervised human mentors (bonus layer, not a dependency)
- Mentors see a queue of submitted (already-blurred) videos
- They can ONLY: leave timestamped comments on videos + assign drills from the library
- **No DMs. No live contact. Guardian account sees every interaction.**
- Mentor sources: HS varsity players (peer mentors, service hours) + verified adult coaches via a partner org

## Safety & privacy design (state explicitly in demo video)
- Video anonymized on-device before upload — raw footage never leaves the phone
- All communication is guardian-visible; report button on every interaction
- "Verified" mentor flag granted only through partner orgs

## CUT LIST (do not build — v2.0 essay material, submission question #6)
- ❌ Virtual little league (teams/rosters/seasons/scheduling)
- ❌ Live virtual practices (async drill assignment only)
- ❌ Open Q&A forum
- ❌ Coach discovery/matching (hand-onboard pilot mentors)

## Pilot plan (September)
One team of ~8 kids for 3 weeks via Mill Creek/Everett Little League, Boys & Girls Club of Snohomish County, or a school program. Deliverables: real usage screenshots, one kid's genuine 3-week progress chart, a quote from a coach/parent.
