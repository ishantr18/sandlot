# Vision

## The problem
Baseball has become a rich kid's sport. Travel ball and private lessons run $3,000–$10,000/year, and quality coaching is increasingly paywalled. Kids without money don't get worse at baseball — they get less feedback.

## One-sentence purpose (use verbatim in the demo video)
Diamond Drive gives underprivileged kids pro-level baseball coaching on any phone — an AI swing coach with privacy built in, backed by supervised volunteer mentors.

## Target audience (required in demo video)
- Youth players (~ages 8–15) whose families can't afford private coaching
- Volunteer mentors, including high-school varsity players (peer mentors)
- Parents/guardians, who can see every interaction

## Why this wins WA-01 (Rep. Suzan DelBene)
1. **Continues the district's winning meta:** WA-01 winners 2023–2025 were all access-equity apps (ViziBox — accessibility; Read Buddy — neurodivergent readers; AnemoDx — health screening for underserved). Sports-access equity is the same DNA in an unused lane.
2. **Privacy engineering aimed at the judge:** DelBene is the House's lead data-privacy legislator. Faces are blurred on-device *before* upload — raw video never leaves the phone. Say this sentence in the video.
3. **Real technical depth:** in-browser pose estimation + biomechanics math, not an API wrapper. The Redmond-corridor bar demands it.

## Judging criteria → how we score
1. **Quality of idea:** equity mission + privacy-first design + peer-mentor model
2. **Implementation (UX/design):** 4 clean screens, progress charts, guardian dashboard
3. **Coding excellence:** on-device CV (pose + face blur), metrics computed from raw landmarks
